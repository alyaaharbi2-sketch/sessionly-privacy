Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what information Sessionly collects, uses, and stores when you use the app.

Summary

Sessionly is designed to work entirely on your device. The app does not require an account, and your session data (timers, programs, and history) is stored locally on your phone — it is not uploaded to our servers unless you explicitly enable an optional cloud backup feature (if and when offered).

Information We Collect

Sessionly does not require you to create an account or sign in to use the app. We do not collect your name, email address, or contact information through the app.

  • Session data (work/rest intervals, number of sessions, history, preferences) is stored locally on your device.
  • Diagnostic data: if the app crashes, basic crash and performance data may be collected automatically through standard platform services (such as Google Play) to help us fix bugs. This data is not linked to your identity.
  • Purchase data: if you subscribe to a premium plan, payment is processed entirely by Google Play. Sessionly does not see or store your payment card details.

How We Use Information

  • To run the app's core timer functionality on your device.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app stability.
  • To manage premium subscriptions through Google Play Billing.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share session data with third parties. Limited, non-identifying crash/diagnostic data may be processed by our infrastructure providers (such as Google Play services) solely to operate and improve the app.

Data Storage & Security

Your session data stays on your device by default. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect any data that is processed. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Sessionly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

  • You can delete all local app data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage from your device settings.
  • You can cancel a premium subscription at any time through Google Play.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
